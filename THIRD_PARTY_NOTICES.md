The character templates/examples, UI skins and symbols, 5×7 bitmap alphabet, terrain textures/blocks, sword artwork, beacon, code and documentation are authored for this project and covered by the repository's [MIT license](LICENSE). Combined examples retain the underlying artwork's stated license.

## Fonts

The website bundles the 12px proportional and monospaced Simplified Chinese WOFF2 fonts from [Fusion Pixel Font by TakWolf, release 2026.10.25](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font/releases/tag/2026.10.25). Both include Latin and Chinese glyphs and are distributed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](site/assets/fonts/LICENSE-OFL).

The original font files and license are included in `site/assets/fonts/` and copied into the published site during its build.
